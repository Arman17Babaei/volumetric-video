Traffic Flow:
------------
All clients stream DASH video simultaneously from the server.
They compete for bandwidth at the bottleneck (server link).

h1 ─┐
h2 ─┤
h3 ─┼──> s1 ──[BOTTLENECK]──> server
... ─┤
hN ─┘

Each client independently:
1. Requests video segments from server
2. Adapts bitrate based on network conditions
3. Logs playback metrics to its own subdirectory
